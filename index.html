About Me

Hi. My name is Nkosiphendule Jwara. I am a dedicated Software Engineer specializing in Mobile and Backend Development. As a proud Mangosuthu University of Technology (MUT) Alumni, I have cultivated a deep passion for creating innovative solutions through technology.

Currently, I am making an impact at eThekwini Municipality, where I work as a Trainee Applications Engineer on critical municipal systems, one of which where I maintain and enhance an application directly serving over 200,000 users. My journey in software development is driven by a commitment to continuous learning and delivering high-quality solutions that solve real-world problems.

I enjoy solving diverse problems, developing solutions that have positive effects on peoples lives and coding scripts that make my everyday life easier and automate repetitive tasks. I also enjoy attending tech events where I engage and connect with other like minded individuals and industry leaders.
