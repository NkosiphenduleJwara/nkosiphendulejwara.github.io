Account & data deletion

Delete My Account

Ask for your account and personal data to be deleted from any app published by developer Nkosiphendule Jwara (tech.nkosiphendulejwara.*). You don't need to have the app installed.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below with the email address you use in the app.
  2. We may email you from that address's inbox to confirm the request really came from you.
  3. Once confirmed, your account and data are deleted and we email you to let you know it's done.

Using the app? The Delete my account button in the app's settings brings you straight to this page with the app already selected.

🗑️ What we delete

  • Your account and login
  • Your name and email address
  • Everything you created in the app (for example workout logs, plans, bookings, rewards)
  • Photos and files you uploaded

🗄️ What we may keep

  • Records we must keep by law, such as payment records
  • Support emails and server logs, for up to 24 months
  • Anonymous usage statistics that can't identify you

⏱️ How long it takes

We delete your data within 30 days of confirming your request. Copies in backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy. Only want some of your data removed but want to keep your account? Choose that option below.

Deletion request form
