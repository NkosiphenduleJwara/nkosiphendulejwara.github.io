Opti Gainz Track AI

The gym app I built to solve my own training problems, and that I'm getting ready to release.

← All projects

  • Cross-platform mobile
  • AI workout plans
  • Workout logging
  • Macro tracking

Why I built it

I used to write my workout logs in my phone's Notes app. It worked, but it couldn't tell me what to do next, and tracking what I ate was a separate mess. I built this app to fix the problems I had in my own gym journey.

What it does

  • Workout logs: stores every session I used to keep in Notes.
  • AI workout plans: generates a plan by analysing my profile, past workouts and other data insights.
  • Nutrition: helps me log, track and analyse the macros of the food I eat.

Where it's at

I'm learning a ton from building it, and I've seen a big improvement in my physique since I started using it. Many people around me face the same problems, so I'm preparing to release it on Google Play and the App Store.
